Case Studies

Real engagements. Measured outcomes.

Six examples from our archive — clean-ups, restructurings, software deployments, and bank-grade reporting that unlocked the next chapter of growth.

01 / Hospitality

KDT Hotels & Suites Limited

The hotel runs a club house and offers accommodation and lodging services. By any standard it is a very successfully run enterprise, but the business started without a financial control function. The owner, having physical control over banking and purchase transactions, was satisfied with daily and weekly reviews to be sure the hotel's funds were not being stolen. The company kept up this practice for close to two years — until it was time for tax audit.

Challenge

The challenge was to get records prepared in a format that represents the company's financial position and supports the tax filings or reports that would be suitable for the tax auditors' review and give them a basis to assess the appropriate taxes.

Solution

The managing director contacted us to help put the records in order. The first thing we did was clean up the records. We appealed to the tax auditors for time and began the process of preparing the records from the bank statements and the available source documents. It was a herculean task — the records were incomplete and the revenue agencies were dangling heavy penalties. We dug into the archives to straighten out the books and attended to the tax auditors.

Result

We attended to the tax auditors and negotiated the fines and tax payable. We took on the bookkeeping, accounting, and taxation services upon the conclusion of the audit. Since then, tax filing, audit response, and CRA correspondence have been seamless. The company passed its tax audit, fines and penalties were reduced and paid, and the company is now tax compliant.

When I was confronted with tax queries and penalties because I had no adequate accounting records for tax review, I engaged Lucky Idiake and Company to help clean up our books and attend to the tax auditors. To my greatest surprise, the heavy penalty the revenue authority was dangling at my company was substantially reduced and my company was passed for tax audit. Since then, Lucky Idiake and Company have been given the mandate to handle all our accounting, tax, and bookkeeping services — and I am left with more time to focus on our core business.— Bamidele Denis
02 / Quick-Service Restaurants

Astrolux Limited

Astrolux Resources owns Sweet Delite, which runs a fleet of fast-food restaurants. The managing director handles the finance function, corporate image, and human resources, supported by her managers and other employees. Startup capital was drawn mainly from savings. The business started well and within six months grew significantly, with established outlets.

Challenge

Suddenly there was a drop in patronage and revenue began to shrink. The owner and her staff tried to figure out what was responsible and initiated some studies — but it soon got out of hand and the investment was gradually deteriorating.

Solution

The proprietor was advised to engage a consultant to look into operations and identify what needed to be reviewed in order to reverse the course of things. We were contacted for a preliminary discussion and review, then engaged. After our initial meeting and walk-through of the business process, we found that the company had enjoyed the initial patronage because they were new and customers wanted to taste what was on offer. Once that wave passed, the bigger players in the industry responded by modifying their restaurants, introducing promotions, and adding investment — a strategy that worked strongly against Astrolux. We advised a change in business strategy since Astrolux could not compete head-on with the giants. The new process led to: (i) increased product variety; (ii) reshaping of the restaurants — adjusted ratio of customer-service space to food-preparation space, and modernization of the outlets; (iii) introduction of new technology and methods of food preparation; (iv) proper planning and supervision.

Result

The cumulative effects of these measures were increases in customer perception, patronage, revenue, and profitability.

Upon realizing that our business — which took off initially very well — began to nose-dive, we checked to see what we were doing wrong and tried to make amends. The more we changed the process, the more things turned south, until we were recommended to speak with Mr. Lucky Idiake of Lucky Idiake & Company. Six months after Lucky Idiake and Company's engagement, we noticed we were able to arrest the downward turn of events. We implemented his recommendations under the strict supervision of his staff. The result was that our company affairs turned around.— Lucy Chimeka
03 / Distribution & Trade

IDI Embroidery Limited

IDI Embroidery Limited is in the business of flooring. The company acts as middleman between several flooring-materials producers and end users. The managing director started the company alone, as there were no funds to engage other employees. When he received an order for materials, he would immediately consult his suppliers and tailor his sales to the supplier's terms. He got the business started and made progress within eight months. He then engaged staff remunerated partially on fixed pay and partly on commission based on sales. The business was largely unorganized in terms of record-keeping.

Challenge

While he was getting good bargains from some suppliers, others were not as profitable. The volumes of sales from each supplier were not uniform, and tracking sales by agent, supplier, or product was an issue. He wanted to know which supplier's product was more profitable so he could re-jig his pricing system. This could not be done because accurate records of sales and cost of sales were not available. Sales analysis by product and supplier was difficult. He decided he needed help from an accounting professional.

Solution

The first thing was to recommend that he use the QuickBooks accounting package to maintain his records. After subscribing, his products and services were analyzed and entered into the system. All past sales were cleaned up and posted accordingly until the records were up to date. Thereafter, invoicing was done on the system in real time with proper classification.

Result

Analyzing products by customer, supplier, class, and other dimensions could be done within minutes at any time. Product profitability and any other analysis was easy, and decision-making became simpler than before.

After about eight months in business, I could not ascertain the direction the business was going or what I needed to change to enhance sustainability and profitability — there were no accounting records, as I could not keep pace with record-keeping. I began a search for an accounting firm to help resolve this challenge. I contacted Mr. Lucky and discussed the issues with him. We soon agreed to work on it together, and within two months the records were straightened out. Now I can analyze my sales by product, customer, and class — and based on the information, I can either review my pricing or drop some products from my stock list.— Job Idiakhoa
04 / Energy & Manufacturing

Emcast Works Limited

Vision Quest is an Energy, Marine, Engineering, and sand-stockpiling-and-selling enterprise. The company obtains contracts and spends most of its revenue on equipment rental. Vision Quest's clients are happy with the work and were prepared to support the company in the acquisition of equipment, so it could keep some of its profit within the business for expansion.

Challenge

When the company sought a bank loan to support the down payment for some operating equipment for mining and manufacturing, the managing director discovered he had not produced the reports the bank required to assess the company's suitability for financial support. When he first approached the bank, the records were inadequate — incomplete and not professionally prepared. His application was delayed and was on the verge of being rejected because the books were not comprehensible.

Solution

When our firm was contacted, we obtained his bank statements and other documents. We deployed QuickBooks software, input the records diligently, and produced professionally prepared financials with notes. The company's application was retrieved and restructured.

Result

The bank reviewed the accounts and was satisfied. The loan was approved and the letter of credit was established. Since then, Lucky has been the company's accountant and handles all the accounting, bookkeeping, and taxation functions.

I was at the point of losing my deposit because the purchase contract was almost being breached when Lucky came to the rescue. I explained my plight and the situation of the records to him. Lucky set to work immediately and began to clean the books of accounts, preparing professional accounts that reflected our business transactions. Within a week, the accounts were ready and presented to the bank for review.— Peace Idiakhoa
05 / Bakery

Bon Apetite Limited

Bon Apetite was conceived to produce good-quality bread and pastries at competitive prices, provide a great and conducive working environment full of fun for both customers and employees, and treat everyone with respect and dignity.

Challenge

When Egua set up this company after several years of working career, she set her objectives to run a company with human feeling and yet be successful. She did not engage an accountant because she believed the experience she had garnered through the course of her work would take her through. She made progress at the beginning until she discovered that her entire time was being spent trying to keep the accounting records. In spite of this, the books were not being maintained according to standards as she struggled to carry out analysis of financial performance, and the business began to suffer. Debts were accumulating and creditors continued to grow. There were multiple settlements of invoices because everything was done on spreadsheets and manually. In order to save her investment, she decided she had to hand off financial record-keeping to capable hands and concentrate on her core competence. Our firm — being the business plan developer — was contacted to review the challenges and help out.

Solution

We noticed she had left her area of competence to take on the accounting and bookkeeping functions. Controls had been neglected and the main business drivers ignored. We took over the financial functions, automated record-keeping by deploying QuickBooks accounting software, and introduced serial numbering of invoices. Multiple invoices and duplicate bills were flagged by the system and eliminated. We generated timely and accurate records of operations for her to make the necessary decisions. In no time, she was able to bring the business back on track to meet her goals and recoup her investment.

Result

The business is up and running, very profitably, and the objectives are now being realized.

Lucky took over the accounting and bookkeeping services and gave me an opportunity to focus on developing the business and monitoring the achievement of our initial objectives.
06 / Non-Profit

Catholic Men Organization, Lekki Deanery

The Catholic Men Organization is a Christian organization that takes the lead in the management of the church to ensure the smooth running of the Catholic Church. The organization spearheads the raising of funds for the various projects of the church and ensures that the officiating priests are adequately taken care of.

Challenge

Until 2017 this deanery took the back seat as it encountered difficulty raising the necessary funds for the upkeep of its officiating priests and to support its activities. Interest waned and the organization lagged in several aspects.

Solution

In 2017 a new executive was elected and Lucky was made the Financial Secretary of the executive team. A brainstorming session was held and we came up with the idea that the principles of marketing a not-for-profit organization should be adopted in revamping the deanery — increasing perception, wiping out the negative image of the organization, and creating a new one in order to attract more members and increase commitment. To achieve this, we identified strong leadership, recognized opinion-molders, introduced structural reforms, and initiated participatory monthly meetings with mini celebrations. We started membership empowerment programs, provided easy feedback access, organized and participated in life-improvement events, sponsored and participated in humanitarian projects, demonstrated fairness in dealings with other societies in the church, and maintained good financial records.

Result

Within a short time the organization began to raise substantial sums of money and regained its pride. Projects were funded, monthly seminars were held, and assistance was extended to the less privileged. Membership doubled and monthly meetings became lively once again. Officiating priests are now properly taken care of.

Outcome

Membership doubled, and monthly meetings became lively once again.
